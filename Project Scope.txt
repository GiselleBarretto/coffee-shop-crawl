What is Mojibake?
In Japanese "Moji means 'characters' and bake means 'to become garbled or transformed.' Mojibake refers to garbled text caused by incompatible character encodings between different programs, tools, or operating systems."

Value Score - Custom Score
It's a score that combines ratings, review volume, and price level to determine the best value coffee shops in the area.
Ratings - 80 points
Reviews - 20 points. Reviews do not directly measure quality; they support the rating given to a restaurant. We take the logarithm of reviews. 
Price - We penalize price by 8 points per additional price tier.
